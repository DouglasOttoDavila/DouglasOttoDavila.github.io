## The quality problem

A failed test is a signal, not a diagnosis. Teams lose time when every failure enters the same investigation queue.

## The proposed system

The classifier explores how historical execution data, logs, timing, and environment evidence could support triage. It is presented as a proposal and prototype rather than a production claim.
