Privacy

This site uses OAuth sign-in (GitHub or Google) via Supabase Auth to gate protected pages. No passwords are stored by this portfolio.

What is stored

  • Auth session data handled by the provider and Supabase.
  • Your email address may be used for access control (allowlist) if enabled.

What is not stored

  • Your provider password.
  • Any portfolio-local password or PIN.
