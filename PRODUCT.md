# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters, hiring managers, QA and engineering leaders, and peers interested in Douglas D'Avila's professional profile and portfolio. They need to understand his expertise, credibility, and career trajectory quickly, then examine concrete evidence through projects and working demonstrations.

The portfolio also serves practitioners interested in AI-driven solutions for quality assurance, software development, and the wider software delivery lifecycle. These visitors should be able to explore embedded tools as credible product experiences without losing their place in the broader professional narrative.

## Product Purpose

Present Douglas as a QA architecture and automation leader who applies AI pragmatically across software delivery. The site should convert professional experience into clear, inspectable evidence: career context, technical projects, documented thinking, and interactive AI-driven demonstrations.

Success means a recruiter can identify Douglas's role, value, and strongest evidence within seconds, while a technical visitor can go deeper and leave with confidence in both his engineering judgment and execution ability.

## Brand Personality

Precise, kinetic, assured.

The brand should feel technically advanced and unmistakably current without performing a generic idea of “AI.” Its voice is intelligent, direct, and grounded in demonstrated work. Motion and interaction may create energy, but clarity and professional confidence remain dominant.

## Anti-references

The site must not resemble generic AI-generated portfolio templates or fashionable “AI” landing pages assembled from neural gradients, interchangeable glass cards, decorative particle fields, fake terminal theatrics, robotic copy, or excessive loading sequences.

It must also avoid immersive interaction that delays access to projects, hides core content, or turns navigation into a puzzle. Visual spectacle cannot outrank recruiter comprehension, accessibility, or performance.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence before claims.** Use working demonstrations, project artifacts, and specific outcomes to prove expertise.
2. **Recruiter clarity in seconds.** Make Douglas's role, value, and strongest work immediately legible.
3. **Motion explains the system.** Use animation to reveal relationships, state changes, and flow rather than as ambient decoration.
4. **Advanced technology stays hospitable.** Sophistication must never obstruct navigation, readability, accessibility, or performance.
5. **One identity, multiple depths.** Embedded tools should feel like credible products while remaining recognizably part of the portfolio.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Support complete keyboard operation, visible focus indicators, semantic structure, readable contrast, and states that do not depend on color alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences with equivalent low-motion or instant interactions. Keep core content understandable and usable when enhanced motion or scripting is unavailable.
